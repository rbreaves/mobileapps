**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to Bucky, an Android keyboard, sidebar, and productivity app developed by Ryan Reaves.

Effective date: 2026-06-08

**Short Version**

Bucky is designed to work on your device. Bucky does not collect, record, sell, share, or transmit your personal information. Bucky does not collect keystrokes, typed text, passwords, messages, clipboard contents, IP addresses, device identifiers, analytics, crash reports, location, contacts, photos, files, or browsing activity.

**Information Bucky Does Not Collect**

Bucky does not collect or send:

* Keystrokes, typed text, passwords, messages, or text entered through the keyboard
* IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or network information
* Names, email addresses, account details, contacts, location, photos, files, or clipboard contents
* Analytics, usage metrics, crash reports, session recordings, or tracking events
* A list of apps installed on your device
* Screenshots or screen contents

Bucky does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, cookies, or similar tracking technologies.

**Keyboard Input**

Bucky is an Android input method, so Android allows it to send keys and text into the app you are using. Bucky uses that access only to provide keyboard features, hotkeys, shortcuts, and related input behavior. Bucky does not store, log, analyze, upload, or transmit what you type.

**Local Settings Stored On Your Device**

Bucky stores settings locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences. These settings may include keyboard appearance, sidebar placement, pinned state, app launcher shortcuts you choose, hotkey profiles you create, Pomodoro settings, premium entitlement state, and similar configuration.

These settings stay on your device unless you choose to export them, import them, use Android device transfer, or use Android backup. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android or Google may back up app settings according to your device and account settings. That backup is controlled by Android/Google, not by Bucky, and is not sent to Ryan Reaves.

**Installed Apps Access**

Bucky may ask Android for installed launcher apps so you can choose apps to place in Bucky's app launcher or create app-specific hotkey profiles. This information is used on-device to show choices and launch apps you select. Bucky does not transmit your installed-app list.

**Overlay, Shizuku, And Screenshot-Based Blur**

Bucky uses Android overlay permission to show its floating keyboard/sidebar UI.

Some optional features can use Shizuku if you grant permission. For example, Bucky may use Shizuku to change device night mode or to create a blurred backdrop from a screenshot for the pseudo-blur visual effect. Screenshot data used for pseudo blur is processed in memory on your device for display only. Bucky does not upload, sell, share, or store those screenshots as files.

**Purchases**

Bucky uses Google Play Billing for premium features. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Bucky checks purchase status through Google Play Billing and stores only local entitlement state needed to unlock premium features. Bucky does not collect your payment card, billing address, or Google account details.

Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play purchases.

**Internet, Analytics, And Third Parties**

Bucky does not request Android's Internet permission and does not operate a server for collecting app data. Bucky does not send analytics or personal data to Ryan Reaves.

Bucky may open external apps or websites only when you choose an action, such as opening the Shizuku download page or starting a Google Play purchase flow. Those external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

**Children**

Bucky is not directed to children under 13. Bucky does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Security**

Because Bucky does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no Bucky server-side personal data store to secure. Local settings remain on your device and are protected by Android's app sandbox and your device security settings.

**Data Retention And Deletion**

Bucky does not retain personal data on developer-controlled servers. Local settings remain on your device until you change them, clear app data, uninstall Bucky, or Android removes them.

To delete local Bucky settings, use Android's app settings to clear Bucky's app data or uninstall Bucky.

**Changes**

This policy may be updated if Bucky's behavior changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.

**Contact**

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact Ryan Reaves at rreaves.apps@gmail.com.
